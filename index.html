🚩 Common Scam Red Flags

1️⃣
Unexpected message

You receive contact from an unknown person or organization.

2️⃣
Urgent or threatening language

"Act now!" "Your account will be closed!" "Confirm immediately!"

3️⃣
Request for OTP/PIN

Never share your One-Time Password or Personal Identification Number.

4️⃣
Request for money

Asking you to pay a fee or transfer money for any reason.

5️⃣
Suspicious link

URL looks different from the official website or shortened links.

6️⃣
Too-good-to-be-true offer

Winning a prize you never entered, unrealistic salary offers.

7️⃣
Unknown sender

Email addresses, phone numbers or profiles don't match official ones.

🚨 I've Been Scammed - What Now?

Emergency guide: Step-by-step actions to take immediately.

Do This IMMEDIATELY:

1

Stay Calm - Stop Communication

Do not reply to the scammer. Stop all communication immediately.

2

Contact Your Bank (If Money Lost)

Call your bank's customer care using the number on the back of your card. Use official contact numbers only.

3

Report the Fraudulent Transaction

Ask your bank to initiate a chargeback or dispute on unauthorized transactions.

4

Preserve Evidence

Keep screenshots, messages, transaction details, and conversation records.

5

Change Your Passwords

If they know any of your account information, change passwords for email, banking, and social media accounts.

6

Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Immediately enable 2FA on all important accounts (email, bank, UPI apps, social media).

7

Block & Report the Scammer

Block the sender on WhatsApp, SMS, email, or social media. Report them to the platform.

8

File an Official Complaint

Report to Cybercrime Portal (cybercrime.gov.in in India) or your local law enforcement.

❌ Do NOT Do This:

❌

Don't pay more money hoping to recover your loss.

❌

Don't ignore it - time is critical for financial recovery.

❌

Don't delete evidence - keep all communications and screenshots.

❌

Don't share your experience publicly in a way that reveals your details.

❌

Don't contact unknown recovery services - they may be scams too.
